Why Choose CBE

Australia's Toughest Bull Bars

Family owned and operated since 1978. Every bar designed, built and tested right here in Adelaide.

🛡️
ADR 84/00 FUPS Certified
Every FUPS-rated bar carries a Component Registration Number (CRN) from DOTARS. Tested to 8 tonnes corner load and 16 tonnes lateral — deflecting just 20–30mm vs the 400mm standard.
📡
Radar & AEB Compatible
Our engineering department has developed radar-aperture designs compatible with Active Brake Assist and Adaptive Cruise Control systems across all major brands.
🇦🇺
100% Australian Made
Not imported. Every bull bar is designed, cut, welded and polished at our factory at 3 Capelli Road, Wingfield. Supporting local manufacturing since 1978.
⚙️
Hi-Tensile Aluminium
6061-T6 high-tensile aluminium uprights (6mm plate), heavy-wall horizontal pipe — no butt welds, drilled and inserted construction for maximum integrity.
🎨
Custom Finishes
Polished aluminium standard. Powder coat in any colour — including Arkote and Shingleback coatings. Polycarb headlight covers and mesh available on request.
🔩
Tow Hitch Standard
Every bull bar comes with a tow hitch as standard fitment. Exact-fit chassis mounting for every make and model — no drilling, no guesswork.
