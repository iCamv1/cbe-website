# Updating the bull bars on the CBE website

**Admin link:** https://chris-barron-engineering.netlify.app/admin/
**Passcode:** James will give you this over the phone. Keep it to yourself, anyone with it can change the website.

Works on a computer, tablet or phone. Whatever you save is live on the website in about 2 minutes.

---

## Adding a new bull bar

1. Open the admin link and type the passcode. You stay signed in on that device for a month.
2. Hit **Add a bull bar**.
3. **Photo**: hit **Choose photo** (or **Take photo** on a phone), or drag a photo into the box.
   Any size is fine, it gets shrunk automatically. Landscape shots of the whole bar on the truck look best.
4. **Make** and **Model**: pick from the lists. If the model isn't there, hit **Add a new model**,
   type the name (e.g. `T680`) and hit **Add**.
5. **Title**: what customers see, e.g. `Volvo FH FUPS Polished Bullbar`.
6. **Description**: a few lines on fitment, FUPS rating, finish, what's included.
7. Hit **Save bull bar**. You'll get a message, and the badge up the top says
   "Publishing… about 2 min" until the website has caught up.

## Replacing an old photo

1. Open the bull bar (search for it, or pick the make and model up the top).
2. Hit **Replace photo**, pick the new one (or **Take photo** on a phone).
3. Hit **Save changes**. New photo's on the website in about 2 minutes, old one's gone.

## Changing or deleting one

1. Find it: pick the make (and model) up the top, or type in the search box.
   **Missing a photo** shows the bars that still need one.
2. Hit the bull bar, change what you need, hit **Save changes**.
3. To delete it, hit **Delete** (bottom left), then **Yes, delete it**.

## Good to know

- **Order on the website**: bars are listed A to Z within a model. To put one first, open
  "Display order (optional)" and give it a low number like 10.
- **New brand (make)?** That needs a small website change for the menu and logo. Flick me the brand name and logo.
- **Nothing changed after 5 minutes?** Refresh the website (Ctrl + F5 on a computer). Still nothing, give me a yell.
- **Stuffed something up?** Every save is kept, I can put any earlier version back.
- **Shared computer?** Menu (three dots, top right) then **Sign out** when you're done.
