Welded to Outlast Your Truck

Every Chris Barron Engineering bull bar is a masterclass in construction methodology. We don't cut corners on materials or process. From the moment stock leaves our CNC machines to the final hand-finished weld, every bar is engineered to survive the harshest outback conditions for decades.

  • ✓ 6061-T6 high-tensile aluminium uprights (6mm plate)
  • ✓ Heavy-wall horizontal pipe (not light-wall)
  • ✓ Drilled and inserted joints — no butt welds
  • ✓ CNC-precision cut components
  • ✓ Hand-finished TIG welds
  • ✓ 100% Australian fabrication
Our Standards

Our Commitment to Excellence

No Butt Welds

All joints are drilled and inserted — the strongest possible connection method.

Hi-Tensile Uprights

6mm plate 6061-T6 aluminium uprights — similar strength to hi-tensile steel but significantly lighter.

Heavy-Wall Pipe

Horizontal members use heavy-wall pipe — not the light-wall found in cheaper imported bars.

Tow Hitch Included

Every bar comes with a factory-fitted tow hitch as standard — no extras, no surprises.

Polished Finish

Machine-polished to a gleaming shine. Powder coat available in any colour.

Exact Chassis Fit

Custom-engineered for each specific make and model — bolt-on fitment, no drilling.

Radar Apertures

Purpose-designed cutouts for AEB and ACC radar systems — no improvised modifications.

FUPS Compliance

DOTARS-certified CRN for every FUPS-rated bar. Tested far beyond the minimum standard.

CNC Fabrication

2020 investment in CNC technology ensures sub-millimetre precision across every component.

Our Wingfield Factory

Where precision meets tradition. CNC-cut, hand-welded and polished under one roof in Adelaide.
